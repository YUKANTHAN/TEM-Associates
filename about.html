Who We Are

Company Overview & Legacy

We are pleased to introduce ourselves as one of the growing companies in the field of Design/Development/Construction with 15 years of experience and expertise. TEM has the resource to execute construction projects of large magnitude and technological complexity in any part of India.

TEM is equipped with requisite and wide range of experience to undertake Engineering Procurement and Construction (EPC) projects as well as projects on item rate basis. Contracts are executed using project management techniques.

TEM ASSOCIATES is also Project Management Consultancy specialized in providing Project Management services for sectors like Roads & Bridges, Buildings, Irrigation, Railways, Solar Energy and Waste Management.

TEM ASSOCIATES also provides various In-house ERP Solutions for effective system development and implementation at various stages (Planning, Construction and Project Closure).

TEM ASSOCIATES focuses on Value Engineering as well as cost reduction techniques in order to enhance Gross margin of the project.
