About Fluor

Through our people, projects and partnerships, Fluor connects businesses and communities to advance economies and employment. We create choice and opportunity on a local and global scale.

Building a better world is our purpose. We care deeply about safety and seek to drive quality and efficiency in all that we do. We behave ethically, inclusively and responsibly for a brighter future.

We support our clients in solving critical challenges. We operate throughout the project lifecycle, from conceptual design, engineering, procurement and construction to the operations and maintenance of facilities.

Clients, partners and communities trust Fluor because we are here for the long term – delivering successful projects of both scale and significance for more than a century. From the spark of an idea to a project's completion, our nearly 23,500 colleagues in more than 40 locations around the globe provide professional and technical solutions with world-class expertise.
